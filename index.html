Ask who pulls the permit before the tank comes off the stand.

Denver homeowners keep getting a trip-only quote, then a surprise permit line, then a unit that is not rated for a mile of altitude. A national form will not write that checklist. This page does.

  • Ask who pulls the permit and when — before work, not after the old tank is gone.
  • Ask whether the trip or dispatch fee credits if you approve the replacement.
  • Ask if the unit is rated for Denver altitude and what venting it needs.
  • Ask whether an expansion tank, pan, and shutoffs are in the written total.
  • Ask for the Colorado master plumber or contractor number you can look up.
  • Prefer a shop that publishes a phone on its own site, not only a Maps pin.
