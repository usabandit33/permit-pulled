Questions people search after the hot water dies

Permit before or after?

A shop that installs first and files later can leave you with a tank that fails inspection. Ask who pulls the permit, which office it goes to, and when the inspector comes.

Does the trip fee credit?

Dispatch and diagnostic fees are the most argued line on a residential invoice. Ask whether that number comes off the install if you say yes the same day.

Altitude rating

Gas water heaters are derated at elevation. A unit sold for sea-level BTU can underperform a mile up. Ask if the model is rated for your altitude and what venting it needs.

Expansion tank and pan

Closed systems often need an expansion tank. Garages and some closets need a pan and drain. Ask if those are in the written total or extra on site.

License lookup

Ask for the master plumber or contractor number printed on the invoice and look it up on your state board before they cut the old tank loose.

Same-day swap vs quote-only visit

Some shops stock common tanks and swap the same day. Others charge a visit just to measure. Ask which kind of appointment you are booking.

Tank vs tankless vs heat pump

Those are different jobs, different permits, and different wait times for parts. Do not treat a teaser tank price as a tankless quote.
